01 — Company Profile

An E&M contractor built
around containment.

公司簡介

An E&M contractor in Hong Kong built around one discipline: getting cables from A to B cleanly, safely and on programme.

Harbourline E&M Engineering Limited is an E&M contractor in Hong Kong, established in 2008. We specialise in cable trunking, floor trunking and electrical installation for commercial towers, healthcare, hospitality, data centres and transport infrastructure — from first technical submission through to commissioning and O&M handover.

Our in-house team combines registered electrical engineers, submission specialists, BIM drafters and directly employed site supervisors. Because containment, cable routing and finishing tolerances are coordinated in-house, interfaces are resolved on the drawing board rather than on site.

We work as a specialist subcontractor to main contractors and directly for developers, with a delivery model built around early involvement, documented approvals and predictable site attendance.

電纜線槽系統

Pre-galvanised, hot-dip galvanised, powder-coated and stainless systems, supplied with factory-formed bends and fittings to match coordinated shop drawings.

Hot-dip galvanised trunking

Heavy-duty containment for plant rooms, car parks and external risers, with a 55 µm minimum zinc coating.

50×50 – 300×100 mm · BS EN 10346

Pre-galvanised trunking

Lightweight, site-friendly containment for dry internal areas, ceiling voids and riser drops.

1.0 – 1.6 mm · 3 m standard lengths

Powder-coated trunking

Architectural finish in any RAL colour for exposed soffits, retail and public circulation areas.

60–80 µm DFT · RAL 9016 / 7016

Stainless steel trunking

Grade 304 / 316L containment for commercial kitchens, laboratories and marine environments.

1.2 mm · brushed or mirror finish

Cable tray & ladder

Perforated tray, wire mesh and ladder rack for power and data routes through plant and riser spaces.

75 – 600 mm widths · HDG or pre-galv

Fire-rated containment

Tested systems maintaining circuit integrity along protected escape routes and fire-fighting shafts.

2-hour integrity · certified systems

01

In-screed trunking

Galvanised duct cast into the structural screed, with adjustable floor boxes set precisely to finished floor level.

60–180 mm depth · BS EN 50085-2-2

02

Underfloor busbar

Tap-off units at 600 mm intervals for flexible workstation layouts and future reconfiguration without re-cabling.

25–63 A · 4-pole tap-off

03

Recessed floor boxes

Brass, stainless or powder-coated lids with 4–12 compartment configurations and sprung cable exits.

IP44 / IK10 · load tested

04

Perimeter & dado trunking

Multi-compartment skirting trunking with integrated power, data and accessory plates for offices and classrooms.

2–4 compartments · powder coated

05

Raised floor service outlets

Fire-rated grommets and service outlets for data centre and dealing room floors, coordinated with air-flow tiles.

2-hour rated · 600 mm grid

06

Cast-in service ducts

Pre-formed ducts and draw boxes fixed to structural reinforcement before the concrete pour.

Ø50–100 mm · coordinated with SE
